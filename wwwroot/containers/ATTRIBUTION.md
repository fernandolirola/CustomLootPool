# Container pictures

The pictures in this folder show the loot containers in the Loot Atlas hover card.
They come from the [Escape from Tarkov Wiki](https://escapefromtarkov.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Loot_Containers)
on Fandom, resized to 160 px and converted to WebP.

Wiki content is available under [CC BY-NC-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/).
`sources.json` lists, for every container template id, the wiki page and the original picture used.
